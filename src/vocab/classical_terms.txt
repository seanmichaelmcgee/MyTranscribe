# classical_terms.txt — classical medical terms for MyTranscribe's classical-term fixer
# (src/classical_terms.py): eponymous signs, tests, manoeuvres, syndromes, diseases, fractures,
# findings, scores and criteria, and organisms. Written for MyTranscribe on 2026-11-20 (MIT, same
# as the repository) from general medical knowledge; not copied from any list.
#
# One term per line, written as it should appear. "term | other" lists another spelling that is
# also right and is left alone (Gray Turner's sign | Grey Turner's sign).
# A term of several words is fixed only where its last word ("sign", "syndrome", "score") was
# heard: the words before it are matched to the closest term here that ends the same way.
# Single words are added to the spelling corrector, which fixes only words that are not English.
