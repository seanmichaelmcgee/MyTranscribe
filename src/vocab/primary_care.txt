# primary_care.txt — MyTranscribe vocabulary for family practice, general internal
# medicine and general pediatrics. Curated for MyTranscribe (MIT, same as the repo).
#
# FORMAT (edit freely; reloaded at app start)
#   ## topic-name | trigger words that switch this topic on, comma separated
#   category: term, term (Brand), term ...
# - A "(Brand)" after a term adds the brand name as its own term.
# - Categories: drug, dx (diagnosis/finding), lab, test (imaging/procedure/screening),
#   vax (vaccine), abbr (abbreviation), other.
# - Topic "core" is always active. Other topics switch on when their trigger
#   words (or any of their terms) appear in what you've just dictated.
# - Spellings: US/Canadian. Both "albuterol" (US) and "salbutamol" (Canada/UK)
#   are listed so either is recognised.
# - Sources consulted for coverage (not copied): AHRQ MEPS-based top prescribed
#   drug rankings, CDC NAMCS primary-care visit diagnoses, CDC/NACI child
#   immunization schedules. Verify anything clinical yourself.
