MyTranscribe Medical: trying the installer on a PC without an NVIDIA graphics card
==================================================================================

What to expect
--------------
- It runs on the processor with a smaller speech model (small.en). On laptop-class cores the
  text came about 2 seconds after a short message in testing, and 4 to 8 seconds after a long
  letter; an older laptop is slower.
- It makes more mistakes than the desktop version, especially in drug names. Read the text
  before it goes anywhere.
- Nothing you dictate leaves the PC, and the installer needs no internet.

1. Install
----------
- Run MyTranscribe-Medical-Setup-<version>.exe (about 0.7 GB).
  - If it came from Google Drive: Drive cannot virus-scan files this large and says so;
    choose "Download anyway".
  - Windows says "Windows protected your PC", because the installer is not code-signed yet:
    click "More info", then "Run anyway".
- Click Install. No administrator rights are needed; it takes about a minute.
- Leave "Start MyTranscribe now" ticked and click Finish.

Updates
-------
- A fix to the app comes as MyTranscribe-Medical-Update-<version>.exe (under 1 MB). Close
  MyTranscribe, run it, click Update. The speech model and your settings stay as they are.
  It only works on a PC where the full installer has been run.

2. First start: setup (about three minutes)
--------------------------------------------
- How you dictate: traditional (you say "period", "comma") or natural speech.
- This computer: it should say that no NVIDIA card was found and that it runs on the processor.
- Microphone: a headset gives much better text than the laptop's built-in microphone. Run the
  check (stay quiet 3 seconds, then read the sentence).
- Start and stop: many Dell laptops need the Fn key for F9. Press your key on that page to see
  whether it registers; if not, choose another key. Ctrl+Alt+Q always works.

3. Try it
---------
- A short message, a longer letter, a list of medications with doses.
- Worth noting:
  - how long after Stop the text appears;
  - mistakes, especially drug names and numbers;
  - whether the laptop gets hot or loud while you dictate.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- In the app: Options -> Show log. The file is %LOCALAPPDATA%\MyTranscribe\mytranscribe.log.
  It records timings and errors, never what you dictated.
- Installer problems: %TEMP%\MyTranscribe-setup.log.
- Check of the installed copy: open PowerShell and run
    & "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MyTranscribe Medical\python\python.exe" "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MyTranscribe Medical\selftest.py"
  Every line should say PASS.

Removing it
-----------
Settings -> Apps -> MyTranscribe Medical -> Uninstall. Your settings and word lists are kept
unless you tick the box to remove them too.
